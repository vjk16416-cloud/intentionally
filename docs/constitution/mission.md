# Intentionally Mission

## 1. Mission

Intentionally exists to help people form genuine relationships through clearer intent, safer discovery, and more meaningful early conversations.

The product should make dating feel calmer, more human, and less performative.

## 2. Vision

Intentionally should become the trusted place for people who want relationships to begin with care, clarity, and emotional safety.

The long-term vision is not to maximise time spent in the app. It is to help the right people leave the app for the right reasons.

## 3. Definition of the Right Person

The right person means someone a user is genuinely compatible with, who shares their values and intentions, and who helps them feel more like the person they want to become.

This definition has three pillars:

- Compatibility
- Shared values and intentions
- Personal growth and emotional wellbeing

## 4. Definition of a Healthy Relationship

A healthy relationship means a connection where both people communicate openly, share values and intentions, encourage each other's growth, feel emotionally and physically safe, bring joy and fun into each other's lives, respect independence and boundaries, and continue to maintain attraction and effort over time.

This definition has seven pillars:

- Open communication
- Shared values
- Personal growth
- Emotional and physical safety
- Joy and fun
- Independence and boundaries
- Attraction and continued effort

## 5. Purpose

The purpose of Intentionally is to reduce the noise, pressure, and ambiguity of modern dating.

Every major product decision should help users understand:

- who they are meeting
- why the connection might matter
- what happens next
- how their safety and dignity are protected

## 6. Why Intentionally Deserves to Exist

If Intentionally disappeared, the world would lose a dating app built for genuine connections, and one that actually cares about its users.

Intentionally exists because many dating apps optimise for attention, not care. The company's role is to help users build genuine connections with people who are right for them.

Caring about users means respecting their emotions, safety, privacy, autonomy, and time.

The product should prove that a dating app can be commercially sustainable while still prioritising user wellbeing.

## 7. Core Values

Intentionality means every product, design, engineering, and business decision should help users move with clearer purpose.

Authenticity means the product should help people represent themselves honestly and meet others as real people, not profiles to optimise.

Safety means emotional and physical wellbeing are core product requirements, not secondary policy concerns.

Respect means users should always feel their boundaries, consent, time, and dignity are protected.

Human connection means the product should deepen meaningful interaction rather than maximise shallow attention.

Personal growth means Intentionally should support people becoming more self-aware, open, and ready for healthy relationships.

Privacy means users should understand and control what they reveal, when they reveal it, and to whom.

Integrity means the company should choose long-term trust over short-term growth, revenue, or engagement.

Evidence-based decisions mean product direction should be shaped by real user behaviour, research, and safety signals, not assumption or vanity metrics.

Calmness means the experience should reduce pressure, noise, and urgency wherever possible.

We build thoughtful technology that helps people find the right person, respects human emotions and relationships, supports people becoming happier together, and is successful when users are delighted to leave because it fulfilled its purpose.

Intentionally believes people can learn and grow. We encourage respectful behaviour through education and thoughtful design, while providing clear accountability for repeated or serious breaches of trust and safety.

## 8. Product Philosophy

Intentionally should be simple, calm, and guided.

The product should slow down moments that are usually rushed and remove friction from moments that need confidence.

Discovery should support judgment, not addiction. Matching should open a meaningful next step, not an unstructured inbox. Chat should be earned through mutual interest and a guided Vibe Check.

When in doubt, improve the existing journey before adding a new feature.

## 9. AI Philosophy

AI should help users express themselves more clearly, reflect more honestly, and navigate the product with less pressure.

AI must not manipulate attraction, simulate intimacy, impersonate users, or optimise for dependency.

AI systems should be explainable enough for users and operators to understand their role. They should support human judgment, not replace consent, agency, or accountability.

## 10. Safety Philosophy

Safety is a design constraint and a product promise.

Intentionally should minimise avoidable harm through verification, controlled reveals, private decisions, clear consent, reporting paths, and thoughtful defaults.

The product should assume that vulnerable moments can happen early. It should never pressure users to reveal, respond, meet, continue, or explain themselves before they are ready.

## 11. Business Philosophy

The business should be built around meaningful relationships, not vanity engagement.

Revenue should align with trust, safety, and successful connection. Intentionally should not depend on keeping users stuck, anxious, or endlessly swiping.

Growth is only valuable when it improves the quality and safety of the network.

## 12. Success Definition

Intentionally succeeds when users feel safer, clearer, and more respected while forming real connections.

Success includes users leaving the app because they found someone worth pursuing.

Success does not mean maximum sessions, maximum swipes, maximum messages, or maximum time spent.

## 13. North Star Metric

The north star is meaningful relationship progress.

A user has made meaningful progress when a mutual match completes a guided Vibe Check and both people privately choose to continue.

Supporting metrics should measure trust, safety, onboarding quality, match quality, Vibe Check completion, and post-Vibe Check continuation.

## 14. Product Principles

Progress over speed. Intentionally encourages meaningful progress towards healthy relationships without pressuring users to move faster than they feel comfortable.

Relationship quality over engagement. Features should help users find the right person, not simply increase time spent in the app.

User control over manipulation. The product may guide, suggest and support, but it should never pressure, trick or remove meaningful choice.

Calm momentum. The app should gently reduce drifting, ghosting and endless ambiguity while preserving user autonomy.

Sustainable value. Intentionally should build a commercially sustainable product without exploiting loneliness, insecurity or addictive behaviour.

Make the next step clear.

Prefer calm guidance over open-ended pressure.

Protect privacy until mutual intent is established.

Design for mobile first.

Use evidence from real users before optimising flows.

Keep the interface emotionally safe, visually quiet, and easy to understand.

Do not add complexity unless it improves trust, safety, or relationship quality.

## 15. Things Intentionally Will Never Do

Intentionally will never optimise for addictive swiping.

Intentionally will never use dark patterns to increase engagement.

Intentionally will never sell or expose private user data as a business model.

Intentionally will never make real user routes public for demo convenience.

Intentionally will never pressure users to reveal more than they consented to reveal.

Intentionally will never treat safety as secondary to growth.

Intentionally will never use AI to impersonate users or simulate consent.

Intentionally will never measure success by attention alone.

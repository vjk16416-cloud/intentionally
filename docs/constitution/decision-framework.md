# Decision Framework

This framework is mandatory for every new feature, AI capability, partnership, and business decision.

Its purpose is to keep Intentionally aligned with its mission: helping people build genuine, healthier relationships through trust, safety, clarity, and intentional connection.

## Evaluation Questions

Every proposal should be scored against these questions before it is approved:

1. Does this help users find the right person?
2. Does this encourage genuine connections?
3. Does this improve relationship quality?
4. Does this respect users' emotions, autonomy, and time?
5. Does this improve safety and trust?
6. Does this align with Intentionally's mission and values?
7. Is there evidence or research supporting it?
8. Is it technically sustainable?
9. Is it commercially sustainable?
10. Could it unintentionally encourage unhealthy behaviour?

## How to Use This Framework

A proposal should not be evaluated only by its expected engagement, growth, or revenue impact.

The strongest proposals improve user wellbeing, relationship quality, safety, and business sustainability at the same time.

If a proposal creates meaningful risk, the team should identify whether the risk can be reduced through design, scope, guardrails, research, or a different implementation.

## Final Principle

If a feature significantly conflicts with Intentionally's mission of helping people build genuine, healthier relationships, it should be redesigned or rejected regardless of potential engagement or revenue.
